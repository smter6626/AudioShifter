## GPL code rights remain intact

The GPL code licence does not grant a licence to use the AudioShifter name,
logo, icon, or official branding. Conversely, this brand policy does not revoke
or narrow the GPL rights to use, modify, redistribute, sell, or commercially
support the covered code.

Fork authors may use and commercially distribute the GPL code, but they must
use their own product name and brand and must not impersonate an official
AudioShifter version.

## Rules for modified versions and forks

Without prior written permission from Yeming Dai, a modified version or
derivative must not continue to use `AudioShifter` as its product or application
name. It must replace:

- the application and product name;
- the bundle identifier;
- the AudioShifter logo and application icon; and
- other names, visual elements, domains, accounts, download pages, or source
  identifiers likely to make users believe it is an official release.

The known tracked AudioShifter brand assets are:

```text
macos/assets/source/audioshifter_icon.png
macos/assets/AudioShifter.icns
```

A modified version must prominently provide this notice, or an accurate
equivalent in the language used by its users:

```text
This is an unofficial fork based on AudioShifter.
It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or supported by the AudioShifter project.
```

Accurate Chinese version:

```text
这是一个基于 AudioShifter 的非官方分支。
它与 AudioShifter 项目不存在隶属关系，也未获得该项目的认可或支持。
```

A fork must not claim or imply that it is an official, certified, authorised,
endorsed, or supported AudioShifter release.

## Permitted descriptive references

Reasonable descriptive or nominative references are permitted when they are
accurate and do not imply affiliation or endorsement. Examples include:

```text
Based on AudioShifter
Forked from AudioShifter
Compatible with AudioShifter output
```

The ordinary text name `AudioShifter` may be used for these references. The
official logo, icon, or confusingly similar presentation must not be used to
make a fork appear official.

## Unmodified official releases

Permission is granted to copy the AudioShifter name, icon, and other brand
assets only as necessary to reproduce or redistribute an unmodified official
release built from an official AudioShifter tag.

This is a narrow brand-assets permission, separate from the GPL. It allows an
official tag to be reproduced without forcing the reproducer to rename an
otherwise unmodified official build. It does not relicense the name, logo,
application icon, or official branding under the GPL.

An official Release may be redistributed unchanged, including its original
AudioShifter name and icon, provided that the distributor:

- preserves the original source attribution, licence materials, checksums, and
  accompanying notices;
- does not alter the application or its Release assets;
- does not imply that the distributor is the project owner, an official mirror,
  an authorised agent, or an endorsed support provider; and
- does not use a misleading domain, account, page, or surrounding presentation
  that creates source confusion.

This permission to relay an unchanged official Release does not authorise use
of the brand for a modified product or a separate service.

Modified versions and forks must replace the AudioShifter name, bundle
identifier, icon, logo, and other source-identifying branding unless prior
written permission has been obtained. They remain free to use, modify, and
commercially distribute the GPL-covered code under their own branding.

## No prohibited GPL restrictions

This policy does not prohibit commercial use, charging money, redistribution,
or modification of GPL-covered code. It manages only branding, official source
identity, and avoidance of user confusion.
